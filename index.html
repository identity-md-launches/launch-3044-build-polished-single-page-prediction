How a round works

  1. Pick Squeek if you think Squeektoshi will be running when the timer ends, or Sleep if you think the wheel will be still.
  2. Your pick locks for 90 seconds while the orange ring around the wheel drains.
  3. When the ring is empty, this page reads the real status and scores the round.

Scoring

A correct pick scores 1 point plus the streak you had going in, so a pick made on a streak of 2 scores 3. A wrong pick scores nothing and resets your streak.

Where the answer comes from

measurement.coreStatus decides. When that word is unclear, rpmMilli decides: 1 rpm or more counts as running. If neither answers when the timer ends, the round is not scored and your numbers stay as they are.
